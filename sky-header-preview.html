The structure (what changed vs the flat white):

1. Headers stay YOURS — every top bar (AirConnect, chat headers, Status, Calls, Settings) keeps the deep-sky #0C4A6E with white text, exactly the color you chose.
2. White is the canvas only — behind chats, lists and settings sits the smart white #F8FAFC. Content breathes; the brand stays strong.
3. Bottom nav is deep sky — the whole app is "framed" top and bottom by your color, with a white canvas in the middle. That's precisely how WhatsApp (green), Telegram (blue) and Signal (blue) do their light themes — globally accepted.
4. Bubbles — incoming: white with a fine border · outgoing: deep sky with white text (your signature).
5. Accents — badges, FAB, links and rings in sky #0EA5E9; semantic colors untouched (accept-green, decline-red, online dot).

Your current app remains the full dark AirConnect Sky design. This page is only the proposal — say the word and I apply it everywhere.
