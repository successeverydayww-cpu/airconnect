🧩 AirConnect Mini Apps

Apps that live inside AirConnect, on their own ring in the Field. Every app is reviewed before it joins.

How it works

Your app runs as its own web app and is embedded inside AirConnect. Once approved, it appears as a tile on the Mini Apps ring in every user's Field. One tap opens your app — no install, no separate download.

Requirements

Secure HTTPSMobile-firstWorks embeddedRespects user data

Your app must be a mobile web app served over HTTPS, load fast on a phone, and run correctly inside AirConnect's frame. It must never break AirConnect's stability, never remove or degrade existing features, and never misuse user data.

Our terms — the essentials

1. Your app and its content must be legal and safe for all ages.

2. No malware, no scraping of AirConnect data, no impersonation of AirConnect or its citizens.

3. Account data, chats and balances stay private — your app sees only what the user gives it.

4. Nothing in your app may break, block or remove any existing AirConnect feature.

5. Commercial terms are agreed at review. Updates to a listed app are re-reviewed.

Submission & review

  1. Build your app and host it on HTTPS.
  2. Send us your app URL, a short description, an icon and your contact.
  3. Our team reviews it against the terms — usually within 3–5 working days.
  4. Once approved, your app goes live on the Mini Apps ring for every AirConnect user.

Every app is reviewed before listing and re-reviewed on every update.
