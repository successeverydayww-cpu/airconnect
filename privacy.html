🔒 Skylar Forge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

1. What we collect

Account basics: your name, email and password (hashed). Workspace content: the apps, data tables and records you build, plus builder chat history. Verification and reset codes. Basic abuse-prevention signals (signup network and failed-login counts).

2. What we do NOT collect

No advertising. No tracking pixels. No selling or sharing of your data with third parties. No reading of your app content for any purpose other than operating the service.

3. Email

We email you only for verification, password resets and essential service notices.

4. Where data lives

Your data is stored in your workspace database on our infrastructure (Cloudflare). Only you hold your workspace key, which grants access to your app's data.

5. Deletion

You may export your data anytime. Contact successeverydayww@gmail.com to have your account and data deleted.

6. Children

Skylar Forge is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version always lives at this page.
