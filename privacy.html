Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

What we store

We keep only what the service needs to connect you:

1. Your display name (you choose it, edit anytime by re-registering)
2. Your country (used for your AirConnect number's country code)
3. Your AirConnect number and friend list
4. Chat messages and call signals (offer/answer/ICE) — held max 6 hours, then deleted
5. Push subscription details (if you enable call notifications) — endpoint URL and encryption keys, used only to deliver call and chat notifications

What we never store

No passwords. No email. No phone number from your SIM. No payment details. No call audio or video — ever.

Calls and messages

Voice, video and chat media travel directly between callers (peer-to-peer, WebRTC) and are encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP). Our servers never see or record your call content. Only the small signaling needed to set up a call touches the trunk, and it is deleted within 6 hours.

Who can reach you

Only people on your friends list can call or message you. Anyone else is blocked automatically. Accounts attempting fraud, hijacking or flooding are permanently banned and all their data purged.

Third parties

We do not sell, share or analyze your data for advertising. Infrastructure providers process data only to run the service (hosting, push delivery). No ad networks. No trackers.

Your control

Uninstalling the app and not using your number removes you from active use. To have your account and remaining data fully deleted, contact us and we purge your node, friends, messages and subscription immediately.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: reach us through the AirConnect app chat or the support channel provided with your invite.
