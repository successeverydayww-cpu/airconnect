AirConnect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains, in full detail, how AirConnect ("we", "the app") collects, uses, stores and protects your information. AirConnect is a private messaging, calling, status and channel platform. We built it on one principle: your life is yours. We do not sell your data — not to anyone, not ever.

1. Who we are

AirConnect is developed and operated by the AirConnect team, founded by Samuel Ufot Amos. For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact us at skylarhenry492@gmail.com — a human reads every message.

3. What we never collect

We do not ask for your contacts list. We do not track your location. We do not read your other apps. We do not build advertising profiles. There are no ads and no third-party trackers inside AirConnect.

4. Who can see what you share

  • 1:1 chats and calls — only you and the person you are talking to.
  • Groups — only members of that group.
  • Status — only your accepted friends, for 24 hours.
  • Channel posts — anyone who opens the channel, as with any public feed.

5. Retention — exactly how long we keep things

  • Statuses: 24 hours, then automatically gone.
  • Channel posts, reactions, comments and view counts: kept permanently until the owner of that post deletes it — nothing is ever removed automatically. If you posted it, you can delete it.
  • Chat history: kept so your conversations survive across devices and reinstallations.
  • Account data: kept while your account exists.

6. Your rights

  • Export: you can request a copy of your chat history and data.
  • Correction: change your name, about, photo and passphrase anytime in My Profile.
  • Deletion: you can delete your own posts, statuses and — through account deletion in the app — your entire account and its data.
  • Blocking: block any person anytime; they can no longer reach you.

To exercise any right, use the in-app control or email us at skylarhenry492@gmail.com. We respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

  • Device-bound login: your passphrase plus a device key keeps strangers out, and changing your passphrase requires the current one.
  • Anti-fraud protection: automated rate limits and ban gates stop abuse and spam.
  • Protected viewing: statuses use a protected, watermarked view against saving.
  • Infrastructure: data is served through Cloudflare's global network with its security protections.

8. Children

AirConnect is intended for users aged 13 and above, in line with standard messaging platforms.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
