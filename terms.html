🛰️ AirConnect — Terms & Data Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026. Using AirConnect means you accept these terms.

1. What AirConnect is

AirConnect is a free voice & video calling and chat app that connects you with friends you have both added. Calls go directly between devices (peer-to-peer) wherever possible. Premium features (AI answering your missed calls) are optional paid extras.

2. Your account & phone number

You register with your real phone number, verified by an SMS code. Your number is your identity in the app and is visible to friends you add. Never share your verification code with anyone — AirConnect staff will never ask for it.

3. What we store

  • Your name, country, phone number and account status.
  • Your chats and call signaling data, kept for up to 90 days for delivery and abuse prevention.
  • Attachments you send (kept while your chat needs them).
  • Technical error reports (anonymous, no message content) to fix bugs.
Chat wallpapers, themes and settings stay on your own device — we never see them.

4. AI calls — disclosure

When the AI assistant answers a call on behalf of a premium user, the caller is always told at the start of the call that they are speaking to an AI assistant, and receives a written disclosure in chat.

5. Rules (zero tolerance)

Fraud, hacking attempts, hijacking other people's numbers, spam flooding or abusing the service will get your number permanently banned and all your data purged immediately, with no refund of any premium payment for the banned period.

6. No warranty

AirConnect is provided as-is. It is a peer-to-peer service: call quality depends on your internet connection. We cannot guarantee 24/7 availability of every feature, and we are not liable for losses caused by missed calls or service interruptions.

7. Your choices

You may stop using AirConnect at any time. To delete your account and data completely, contact support through the app — we erase within 7 days. You must be 13+ to use AirConnect.
